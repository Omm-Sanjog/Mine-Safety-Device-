Continuous gas, climate and ground-motion telemetry across the working panel — with a strata forecast that projects present conditions forward before a limit is reached. Choose a data source to begin.

Demonstration

Simulated panel

A modelled bord-and-pillar panel with six nodes and drifting gas and ground-motion behaviour. Use for demos, training, and seeing how the forecast responds.

  • Auditable hazard model
  • Injectable fault scenario
Field

Live gateway

Connect to a VAYU gateway on the mine network. Nodes appear as they report in; panels stay empty until real telemetry arrives.

Readings are advisory. Statutory gas testing and record-keeping remain the duty of the appointed competent person.
